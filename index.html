Buying IT for Your Practice: What Do You Really Need?

Jenny Jarvis SOLSTAS


About this session

Before investing in new technology, how do you know what your practice really needs? This webinar explores how to identify your requirements, assess whether new hardware, software or digital health technologies will work with your existing systems, and evaluate the security, privacy, costs and ongoing support considerations. Drawing on RACGP 6th Edition F10 and the Australian Digital Health Agency's Digital Health Procurement Guidelines, we'll work through a practical assessment process using online patient forms as an example. You'll see how to compare potential solutions, weigh up the options and make an informed decision that meets your practice's needs, both now and into the future.

When Your IT Stops Working: Can Your Practice Keep Operating?

Jenny Jarvis SOLSTAS


About this session

Clinical system outages, internet and phone failures, ransomware and other disruptions can quickly affect a practice's ability to deliver services and care for patients. This webinar explores how practices can prepare for technology disruptions, maintain essential operations and recover safely. We'll look at the respective responsibilities of the practice and its IT provider, including incident coordination, alternative workflows, communication, backup arrangements and the safe restoration of services. Using a realistic GP practice scenario, we'll work through the decisions that need to be made, the information and support required from your IT provider, and how to identify improvements that strengthen business continuity.

The Culture Prescription: Creating a Practice Where People, Patients and Performance Thrive

Lindy MacPherson Lindy MacPherson Advisory


About this session

Practice owners are increasingly expected to build accountable, high-performing teams while navigating the complexities of psychological safety, psychosocial hazards and evolving accreditation standards. Yet many are leading people and shaping culture without formal leadership training. In this practical and engaging session, Lindy MacPherson explores how culture influences staff engagement, patient experience and business performance through the simple framework of People, Patients and Performance. Participants will leave with practical strategies to strengthen accountability, create consistency across their teams and build a culture where people, patients and performance can thrive.
